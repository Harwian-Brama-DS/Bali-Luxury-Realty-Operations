The Challenge

Client Scenario

Made Suteja, Principal Broker of Bali Luxury Realty, needed a VA to transform his chaotic operations into a structured, trackable system across 6 coverage areas in Bali.

Pain Points Before VA

  • Fragmented listing data across WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets
  • 35% of client follow-ups were missed or delayed
  • Double-bookings on open house events with no central calendar
  • Zero real-time visibility into deal pipeline worth billions
  • Average closing time of 45+ days with no standard checklist
